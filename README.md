FINGER DRAW
Ever wanted to paint on your screen using just your hand? That's exactly what this is. Point your finger at the camera and draw anything you want, no mouse, no pen, no touch screen. Just you, your hand, and a webcam. It sounds simple but once you try it, it's genuinely hard to put down.


GETTING STARTED
Just open finger_draw.py in any Python IDLE and press Run. The app takes care of everything else on its own. First time you run it, it might take half a minute to get itself ready. After that it opens instantly every time.


HOW TO DRAW
Hold up only your index finger and point it at the camera. That's your brush. Move it around and watch it paint on screen in real time. To stop drawing without putting your hand down, pinch your thumb and index finger together, like you're picking up something tiny. That puts you in hover mode so you can reposition your hand without leaving a mark. Open your fingers back out and you're drawing again.
To erase everything and start fresh, just open all five fingers wide like a high five. The canvas clears instantly.


CHANGING COLOURS
On the left side of the screen you'll see a vertical strip of ten colours. Simply point your index finger at whichever colour you want and it switches immediately. The selected colour gets a white border around it so you always know what you're painting with.


BRUSH SIZE
On the right side there's a slider. Point your finger at it and drag up to make the brush bigger or drag down to make it smaller. You can also just tap the plus or minus keys on your keyboard if that's easier.


CANVAS MODES
At the top of the screen there are three buttons, CAM, BLACK, and WHITE. Point at one to switch. CAM mode lets you draw right on top of your live camera feed which looks amazing. BLACK gives you a dark canvas like a chalkboard. WHITE gives you a clean bright surface. Your drawing stays on screen no matter which mode you switch to.


A FEW TIPS
Good lighting makes a big difference, so try to have your hand well lit. Keep your hand inside the camera frame and make sure your index finger is clearly raised above the others when you want to draw. The pinch works best when your thumb and index tip are actually touching.
That's really all there is to it. Have fun.
